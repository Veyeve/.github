🌱 About

Hey! We're Vey.

Formerly known as Veyeve, we're an independent organization interested in software, technology, open source, and building things simply because we believe they should exist.

We're not trying to be the next giant tech corporation. We're here to make useful things, experiment with ideas, learn along the way, and share as much of our work as we can.

Vey is home to our own projects and companies, each with its own identity and direction. Some things we build are experiments, some are long-term projects, and others are things we hope people will genuinely enjoy using.

We believe the best software doesn't always come from the biggest teams. Sometimes it comes from a few people with an idea, a little curiosity, and the willingness to make something happen.

Our philosophy is pretty simple: build what you want to see in the world, and whenever possible, build it in the open.

💭 What matters to us
🔓 Open source — We believe software is better when people can inspect it, learn from it, improve it, and build upon it.
🧑‍💻 Independence — We value creative freedom and the ability to build things on our own terms.
🛠️ Practical software — We like making things that solve actual problems rather than adding complexity for its own sake.
🌱 Learning by building — Every project is an opportunity to experiment, improve, and discover something new.
🤝 Community — Software is built by people. We appreciate everyone who shares knowledge, reports issues, or contributes.
✨ Doing things differently — We don't believe every project needs to follow the same formula.
🏢 Our Companies

Vey also serves as a home for companies we own and operate.

We enjoy building things under different identities, giving each company the freedom to develop its own ideas, products, and direction.

🚀 What We're Building

We like making things. Sometimes they're small experiments, sometimes they're larger projects, and sometimes they turn into something we continue developing for a long time.

Here's what we're currently working on.

🤖 Open-source Discord Bot

IN DEVELOPMENT · OPEN SOURCE · COMMUNITY SOFTWARE

Our current main development project is an open-source Discord bot.

We're working on building a bot that can provide useful functionality to Discord communities while remaining approachable for developers who want to explore, modify, or contribute to it.

Our intentions are to make something that is:

⚙️ Useful for everyday Discord communities.
🛡️ Helpful with moderation and server management.
🎛️ Flexible enough to accommodate different communities.
🧩 Designed with room for additional functionality.
🔓 Open to inspection and community contributions.
📚 Documented so people can understand and work with it.

<details>
<summary>🤖 Development philosophy</summary>

We don't want to build another unnecessarily complicated bot.

We want to focus on useful functionality, understandable code, sensible design decisions, and an architecture that can grow over time.

We also want developers to be able to understand what the bot is doing instead of treating it like a black box.

More information, installation instructions, features, and release notes will live in the project's repository.

</details>

🔓 Open Source

Open source isn't just something we put on a badge. It's an approach to building software that we genuinely appreciate.

We like the idea that someone can discover a project, look through its code, understand how it works, learn something from it, and potentially make it better.

We want to contribute to that same environment.

What open source means to us
Transparency: People should be able to understand what they're using.
Accessibility: Useful software shouldn't have unnecessary barriers.
Collaboration: Good ideas can come from anywhere.
Freedom: Projects should give people room to experiment and adapt.
Longevity: Maintainable software is more valuable than rushed software.
Not just code

Open-source contributions don't have to involve writing thousands of lines of code.

You can contribute by:

📝 Improving documentation.
🐛 Finding and reporting bugs.
💡 Suggesting ideas.
🎨 Improving design and usability.
🌍 Helping with translations.
🧪 Testing features.
💻 Writing code and submitting pull requests.

<details>
<summary>📋 How to contribute</summary>

Interested in contributing to one of our projects?

Find a repository that interests you.
Read its README and contribution guidelines.
Look through open issues.
Choose something you would like to improve.
Submit an issue or pull request.

Please remember that individual projects may have their own contribution requirements.

We're always happy to see people taking an interest in what we're building.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🔐 Security and responsible disclosure</summary>

If you discover a security vulnerability in one of our projects, please use the repository's private security reporting options where available.

We appreciate responsible reporting and giving maintainers the opportunity to investigate and resolve problems.

</details>
